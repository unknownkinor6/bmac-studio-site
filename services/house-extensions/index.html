Chartered does not mean expensive

Clients say the opposite. On Houzz, where the rating is broken into separate scores, value sits at 5.0 alongside work quality and communication, across eight reviews.

The saving appears on site rather than in the fee. A drawing set that answers the builder’s questions before he asks them cuts variations, delays, and the costs that follow both.

It also means the person designing your extension is accountable to a regulator, carries the professional indemnity insurance that goes with registration, and was trained in how a building goes together — not only how it looks. More about the practice.

Drawings your builder can price

Vague drawings produce vague quotes. When three builders price the same detailed set, you compare like with like instead of guessing which quote has gaps hidden in it.

Detail also settles arguments before they cost money. Junctions, thresholds, insulation lines and structural openings get resolved on paper, where changing your mind is free.

You may not need an application

A good share of extensions can be built without one, and some qualify for a lighter approval route instead. Which of those covers your address gets settled at the start, before any fees are committed. The rules, timescales and exceptions are on the planning permission page.

Refused before?

Extensions do get turned down. We have taken on schemes that failed under a previous designer and seen them approved. What to do after a refusal.

How the work runs

  1. A first conversationAbout what you want, and what the house can reasonably take.
  2. A surveyThe existing house drawn up as it actually is, not as the estate agent drew it.
  3. Design optionsWith build cost and planning risk discussed against each one.
  4. The applicationPlanning or prior approval, submitted and managed for you.
  5. Construction drawingsBuilding regulations and technical detail, ready to tender and build from.

You get answers between those stages too. Houzz lists the practice as responding quickly, and questions during a build do not sit unanswered for a fortnight.

Where we work
