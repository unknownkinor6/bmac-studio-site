Architect is a title protected by law

You may only use it if you are on the ARB register, which requires seven years of education and practice, current professional indemnity insurance, and conduct answerable to a regulator.

Architectural designer is not protected. Anyone can print it on a van tomorrow.

Architect

Seven years of education and practice. On a statutory register. Insured as a condition of registration. Answerable to a regulator if something goes wrong.

Architectural designer

No protected title, no register, no required qualification. Plenty do good work and some are excellent — but the accountability behind the name is not the same, and neither is the recourse.

The work

House extensions, loft conversions, refurbishments, new builds and planning applications. Mostly residential, mostly Kent and south-east London, mostly houses that need to work harder than they currently do.

  • House extensions — rear, side return, two storey and wrap-around.
  • Loft conversions — rooflight, dormer, hip to gable and mansard.
  • Building conversions — garages, barns and change of use.
  • Planning — applications prepared, submitted and managed, including schemes already refused.
  • Technical delivery — construction drawings detailed enough to tender and build from.
