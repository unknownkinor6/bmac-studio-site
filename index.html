02 The practice

Drawn first. Then built.

Every project is a drawing set before it is a building, and the gap between the two is where budgets go.

So the detail goes in early: junctions, thresholds, insulation lines, structural openings. Resolved on paper, where changing your mind is free.

04 How it runs

Five stages, in order.

Most householder projects follow this shape. You can stop after any stage.

Stage 01 First conversation

What you want, what the house will take, and whether it needs permission at all. Free, and sometimes it ends here.

Stage 02 Survey & concept

The house measured and drawn as it stands, then two or three ways of solving the brief, priced for feasibility rather than taste.

Stage 03 Planning

Drawings, forms and the planning statement, submitted and then managed through to decision. Including schemes already refused once.

Stage 04 Building regulations

The technical set: construction details, insulation, fire, structure and drainage. The drawings your builder actually prices.

Stage 05 On site

Tender comparison, queries answered as they come up, and visits while it is being built, if you want them.
